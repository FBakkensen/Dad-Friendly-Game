# A fresh mine's rules, off in play (#653, M15-16)

**Goal:** slice 1's first sliver (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-14-slice-1-design.md`, rules 1 and 2, The save). With the fresh mine switched on in a lab run (`fresh.off: 0`), the mine has one level that never runs out, no depth and no mined-out state, a few workers to start with, and its machines open by what he owns (rule 1's table). The parts that leave play at part 1's switch-on leave these rules too. Play is unchanged: the switch reads 1 outside a sweep, as the line's did (#532). Orders, transport and the Light Tower are #654; the numbers are the lab's (#655); the switch-on and the save are #664.

**Shared modules:** the switch joins the line's in `src/line-switch.ts` (`FRESH_OFF`, `freshMineOn`), and turns the line on with it. No depth is `digStones` in `src/mountain.ts` (the one place stones leave the mountain), with `stonesLeft` and `stonesToNextBand` in `src/scale.ts`. The starting workers are `createMountain` (`src/mountain.ts`). The doors are a table beside the tier table in `src/tiers.ts` (`tierInPlay`, `tierShown`), and a locked door is a new `TierLock` kind in `src/crew.ts` (`tierLock`, which `buyCrew` and `listActions` already read). The tap is `tapYield` in `src/rates.ts`; the vein is `veinReward` (`src/veins.ts`); the Gold rush is `holdForRush` (`src/gold-rush.ts`). The upgrades and boosts that go are `upgradeInPlay` (`src/upgrades.ts`) and `boostInPlay` (`src/boosts.ts`), read by `upgradeUnlocked`, `buyUpgrade`, `src/upgrade-reveal.ts` and `buyBoost`. Engineers wait for the first borer in `promote` (`src/engineers.ts`) and `listActions`. No new module: every rule extends the one that already owns it.

## Rulings (agent-run sitting)

1. **One switch, and it brings the line.** `fresh.off` is a sweep factor like `line.off`: 1 (unset) is today's game, 0 plays the fresh mine. A fresh mine is built on the line (rule 2), so `lineOn()` is true whenever `freshMineOn()` is. The two switches share their leaf, so the tier table, the scale and the rules read them without a cycle.
2. **No depth: the mountain stays full.** Stones dug go to the stock, and `remaining` never moves. So the one level is the Topsoil for good: nothing is mined out, no band is crossed, and `stonesLeft` and `stonesToNextBand` are Infinity. What hung on depth leaves with it, with no code of its own: relics (the shallowest is a million stones down), the pass to the kid (at the bottom), the band arrivals, the workshop's door (a depth), the band's materials, and the rock's lean (the Topsoil has none on the line). Tests pin each.
3. **No tap mines.** `tapYield` is 0 and a dig is refused (spec, What goes). The lab values taps through `tapYield`, so it plays no taps either.
4. **No golden vein and no Gold rush.** A vein pays 0, so a vein tap is refused, and time away holds nothing for a rush.
5. **The upgrades that went are out of play:** Strong arms (the tap), Steady hands (the Light Tower's job), Sharper picks (the tap) and Overtime (the factory's job). Not shown, not listed, not bought. Better boots, the machine upgrades and the marks stay. Grandfather's pick and the vein lamps are expedition finds and workshop recipes, which open in part 3 and are dealt with there.
6. **Nine workers to start with.** The spec says "a few", and prototype version 4, which Flemming said "That is right" to, starts with nine. They count on the worker curve as owned workers always have, so the tenth costs 60 × 1.06⁹. The lab sets the number (#655). The one horse wagon comes with transport (#654).
7. **The doors (rule 1's table).** The cart opens at twelve workers, the hoist with a cart, the drill with a hoist. A part-1 machine is shown from the first minute with what it waits for ("Cart: at 12 workers", as the prototype's stage plates show it): a new lock, `{ kind: "owns", tier, count }`, so `buyCrew` refuses it and `listActions` leaves it out. The borer, the pit train (the locomotive), the Great Machine (the winding engine) and kid workers are out of play in a fresh mine: their doors are the Granite, the Foundry and the School, which come with later parts.
8. **Engineers wait for the first borer** (rule 1's table, "as today"): a promotion is refused without one. Today's game lets a worker be promoted at any time; the fresh mine does not.
9. **Nothing in the save changes.** The fresh mine's save facts (rock dug in all, vehicles, the project) are written with the reset at part 1's switch-on (#664). With the switch on, which copy is ahead falls back to `lastSeen` because no depth is counted; no saved game plays it before #664.
10. **#650 gives nothing to this sliver.** Its gain and staffing changes (`machineGain`, `runSpare`, `stagesLowFirst`) belong to the stage plates and Next (#660) and to the lab (#655); the branch stays for them.
