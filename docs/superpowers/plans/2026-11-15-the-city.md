# The city on slice 1's frame (#662)

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-14-slice-1-design.md`, "The city", "While a part is the end of the game", "What any screen has to meet", the Art section and rule 7; prototype v12 (`366b890`) with its review fixes (`a196897`), pictures in `docs/superpowers/specs/slice-1/` (`first-evening-city.png`, `city.png`). Parent #530. Built inside the frame (#659), beside the mine (#660).

## Rulings (agent-run sitting)

1. **Where it lives.** `city-site` fills the frame's middle on the city's site and hides on the mine's, as `mine-site` does. It paints a pure view, `city-view`, and changes the game only through `progress.dispatch`. The scene is drawn by `city-scene` on a canvas in art px.
2. **One painter.** The drawing helpers the mine's scene has (the noise, a rect, a line, a lamp's glow, a sprite strip's frame, the "z", a heap, the night sky and its hills, the pictures' loader) and its canvas loop move to `scene-paint`, and both scenes use them. The mine looks the same.
3. **The transport plate** is on top of the skyline: "Transport", its drivers ("1 driver"), what it is doing ("serving the city", "on hold", or "idle: no order"), the fleet with the drivers it has of the drivers it takes and what those carry ("1 horse wagon: 1 of 1 driver 9/s"), the drivers between − and + (`job-steps`) and a key for one more wagon in the mine keys' form ("+Wagon · 9/s", its price, "1 to run"). The key buys one with `buyVehicle`: the game has no move that buys several vehicles, so the amount key does not reach it. There is no trucks key in part 1.
4. **The skyline** has three plots: the Light Tower's and two "Vacant plot · unassigned". The tag under a plot names the building and its state ("operational", or the step in hand in lower case).
5. **A building rises** from its own picture: the Light Tower is drawn from the ground up to a height of (step + built in of the step) ÷ 3, behind scaffold, and whole, with its lamp lit, once operational. The share is what is built in, as the order strip counts it. Before anything is built in, and on a vacant plot, the plot has stakes and a rope. The scaffold, the stakes, the sky, the ground and the road are drawn in code (call 52, his to approve).
6. **The site in the scene.** What is on the site lies beside the building as a heap of stone, the mine's heap, as high as the site is full. Up to three builders stand at it: the share that has nothing to build in waits with a "z", as in the mine.
7. **The wagons.** A wagon with a driver drives along the road to the site while transport brings, as full as what it brings of what the driven fleet carries; the wagon's heap of stones shows that share (its picture's top rows). A wagon with a driver and nothing to carry stands on the road, empty, with a "z". A wagon with no driver stands behind the road, empty, with nobody on it.
8. **The project's card** is under the skyline: the building's name, its three steps with a tick on each one done, a bar for each good the step requires with what is built in of it (the order strip's numbers, "Stone 1,200 of 25K"), the order key, the builders between − and + with what each builds in and what is on the site of what it holds ("2 builders ×8/s · on site 400 of 500"), and "On completion: night shift output 45%, up from 30%." (the power only, rule "While a part is the end of the game").
9. **The order key.** Before the order: "Place order", "the whole building", green. Delivering: "Delivering", "arriving 9/s", "building 8/s · 52 min", "tap to hold", amber. On hold: "Resume", "on hold", green. The numbers are `orderNumbers` (`transport`). "Arriving" is always a number, "arriving 0/s" with no driver, never a reason. The time is the step's at these rates: the longer of what transport takes to bring what it still requires and what the builders take to build it in. It is left out when the step cannot finish (on hold with goods still to bring, nobody building). It names no limit.
10. **After the part's last roof** the card names the last building with its three steps ticked, says the order strip's line ("The Light Tower is operational · the city is planning its next building") in place of its bars and has no order key, the transport plate says "idle: no order", the drivers' and builders' − and + stay, and the plots left stay "Vacant plot · unassigned". The lines are the spec's proposal. Flemming was asked them with the city's look as one question, with four pictures (`/mnt/project-files/slice-1/build/662-*.png`), and said yes on 15 Nov 2026 (13:33Z): "i like them". He added that the Light Tower's power seems big, which is for the balancing, not this sliver.
11. **A + with no free worker** shows "No free worker. Hire one, or take one off another job." over the card, as in the mine, and goes when a worker is moved or hired.
12. **No new art.** The Light Tower, the horse wagon and the miner are the game's pictures; everything else is code. The vacant plot's stakes and the site's heap are code, as the mine's heaps are, so no box stands where a picture should be. His yes of 15 Nov 2026 (13:33Z) was to the pictures that show them so; PixelLab pictures for them stay open if he asks.
13. **One of each mechanism.** The bars read `orderStrip`'s requirements (`frame-view`), the step's time is `orderLimit`'s `secondsLeft` (`transport`), read without its limit, the drivers' and builders' − and + are `job-steps`, the keys share the mine's CSS, and the words table (`plural`) is `screen-words`.
