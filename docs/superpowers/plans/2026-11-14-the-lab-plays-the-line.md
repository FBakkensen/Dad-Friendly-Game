# The lab plays the line (#534, M15-4)

**Goal:** the band-free half of #534. With the line on in a lab run (`line.off: 0`), the hands staff the machines to lift the line, the lab reads the line through four report-only measures, and `npm run lab` takes sweep factors, so the report can show `narrowRun` for today's band table and the spec's table A. The tuning is #543. Play is unchanged.

**Shared modules:** staffing stays in `src/staffing.ts` (`staffWith` gains the line's fill) and `staffCrew` in `src/rates.ts` (the one search, #500). What the lab reads off the line is `lineView` and `narrowStage` in `src/line.ts`. The sitting log (`src/lab/sittings.ts`) carries the narrow stage and the zero-gain buys; the decision sample (`src/lab/measure.ts`) carries the line view. The measures are one file each in `src/lab/measures`, their arithmetic in `src/lab/line-view.ts`. `tools/strategy-lab.ts` takes `--factors` like `tools/generations-lab.ts`.

## Rulings (agent-run sitting)

1. **Staffing follows the line inside #500's search.** `staffCrew` weighs each staffing it tries as the hands fill it today (fastest first) and as the line fills it (`fillLine`, once with spare hands on stages above the line and once without). `fillLine` keeps the big machines and picks the one-hand machines to reach the highest level the hands allow, halving on the level; with one one-hand kind per stage, that is exact for the big counts. The line is flat while a stage is empty, so a one-step climb can stall there (the review found 48 → 24 for a first Great Machine, and drops of 6% to 31% in late crews with more than 64 counts): `staffForLine` tries every count of the big machines the hands can staff while there are at most 512, and hands the 8 that move the line most to the weighing. Above 512, #500's climb runs alone; the late sweep reaches 475 counts. Two- and three-kind climb steps were tried and added nothing once it was in. A one-seed lab with the line on took 76 s with 64 and 85 s with 512 (with other work on the machine).
2. **The tended slips are listed, not chased.** With no engineer posted, or one on the Great Machine, and no Grandpa, one more worker or machine never slows the line in the swept crews, early and late. With an engineer on the borer, or with Grandpa, it can slip by up to about 8.5%: the tending floor is a share of the plain sum, so it pays for idle surplus on a stage that is not narrow (one borer topped up by the floor of four surplus Great Machines), and Grandpa's tenth goes by machine group. Weighing 32 line counts instead of 8, or filling once per tending guess, did not change the worst slip. The engineers by stage (#539) end the slips. `docs/DESIGN.md` lists them, and the sim test allows 10% only where something tends.
3. **The sweeps are sim tests.** Early crews (16,416: the three posts, Grandpa or not, up to three Great Machines, and the worker counts where a big machine's crew just fits) and late crews (1,920: up to nine borers and pit trains, six Great Machines and 120 workers), each with seven "one more", in `src/line-staffing.sim.test.ts` (the full tier, about two minutes).
4. **Measures by sitting.** `narrowRun` is the longest run of sittings that open on one narrow stage; `narrowShare` is the largest share of sittings one stage opens as narrow (sittings, not minutes, because a sitting is what the dad sees). `handsCarry` is the median, over samples, of the share of the line the hands carry on the narrow stage. `zeroGainBuys` counts machines bought over a run that the line did not need: each kind bought in a second is weighed alone, against the same moment with that kind set back, so a worker bought with it does not hide it. All four are NaN with the line off, and none has a bound: bounds are Flemming's. Each kind is weighed alone, so two kinds on one stage bought in the same second, or a ×10 buy of one kind, count all or nothing; it is a report-only measure.
5. **The narrow stage on a tie** is the first in rock order (face, haul, lift).
6. **`outputShare` in the line's units.** With the line on, the machines' and hands' parts are scaled so they sum to what the line moves, so the tending and crafted deltas (already line deltas) share their units (the hand-off on #534).
7. **The band table as factors.** Table A is today's table times `layer.3.drill.hardness=1/1.1`, `layer.3.borer.hardness=1.25`, `layer.3.train.hardness=1.1`, `layer.3.cart.hardness=0.8`, `layer.4.borer.hardness=1.25`, `layer.4.hoist.hardness=0.8`. No table is hard-coded here; which band's rock applies stays one input (the mountain's band) to `effectiveRates`, as the cage will need.
8. **The report names its factors** under its title; the JSON keeps its shape.
