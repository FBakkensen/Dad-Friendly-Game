# The line turns on (#536) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The line's rule is the game's rule: the hoist and the pit train are on sale, stones a second is the line's speed with #543's numbers, a Buys row shows the line's real gain (and "+0/s" with room to spare), the cart waits for twelve workers, Ready now picks for the lowest stage, and the band words read Table A.

**Architecture:** The switch (`src/line-switch.ts`, the `line.off` factor) goes, and each `lineOn()` site keeps only its line branch, so there is one rule, not two side by side. The line's numbers move into the tables they belong to (`TIER_ROWS` first prices, the machine growth, `BAND_SCALE`, `LAYER_FAVOURS`). The real gain of a machine buy is one new helper in `src/rates.ts` beside `handsGain` (`machineGain`). Ready now keeps its module and picks by stage through `src/line.ts`.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vitest, the labs.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-14-line-works-village-design.md`, "Step 1: the line" (the rule, Machines, the rock, What he sees), and issue #536.

## Global Constraints

- One rule: no `lineOn()`, no `line.off`, no second table for prices, growth, band scale or the rock.
- Numbers from #543: machine growth ×1.219, workers ×1.06, first worker 60, first Great Machine 96,480,000, Deep Heart band scale 39.204, tough ×0.8, suits ×1.1, machines rated ×3.
- The cart is shown once he owns twelve workers (or a cart); then each machine once he owns the one before it, as on the line's chain; the borer, train and Great Machine keep their depth locks.
- No old-save grant and no welcome line.
- The bays still show machines (cart, drill, borer, Great Machine) until #537.
- Every strict lab row, the generations lab and the pacing sims green with no bound moved. The pacing ledger is rewritten, and the PR says why.

## Review Focus

1. A second cart with exactly twelve workers and one cart reads "+0/s" and "The haul has room to spare", with a dark lamp, and still buys.
2. A stage's first machine never reads +0.
3. Ready now never offers a buy that adds nothing; with nothing that helps, it offers a worker.
4. Ready now and a Buys row agree on what a machine adds.
5. A save with hoists or trains from the lab loads and plays (they were already in the save, #533).
