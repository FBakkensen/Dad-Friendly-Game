# Gold rush Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** After at least an hour away, the first golden vein of a check-in glints within about 20 seconds. A tap pays the vein and starts a Gold rush: the live crew digs ×10 for 90 seconds (#468).

**Architecture:** The rush is one more live crew timer, saved as an end time (`rushUntil`) in the workshop map beside `axleUntil` and `chargeUntil`. `effectiveRates` multiplies it on top of `liveCrewTimer`, so the crew line, the cards, live mining and the lab agree with no new argument. Away time shifts a running rush later (close is pause) or drops it after an hour. The quick vein is screen state in `screen-vein.ts`, armed by a new store snapshot field that reports the last away gap.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vite, Vitest, the strategy lab (`src/lab`), PixelLab for the sparkle.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-gold-rush-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Quick vein: armed by an away gap of at least `QUICK_VEIN_AWAY_MS` = 60 minutes. It glints `QUICK_VEIN_WAIT_MS` = 20 seconds after arming, counted only while no card or drawer covers the mine window.
- Rush: `RUSH_MULTIPLIER` = 10, `RUSH_MS` = 90 seconds. The lab may tune them inside ×5–×15 and 60–120 s. No lab bound moves.
- Live only: `effectiveRates` without `now` (away pay) never sees the rush. Taps and the vein's own pay stay unboosted.
- The rush multiplies on top of the strongest crew timer. It does not join strongest-wins.
- At most one quick vein per away gap. A reopen within the hour arms none.
- Never punishing: no price, no streak, no nag, no confirm.
- Numbers rule: real numbers only (×10, the clock). No payback, no "?".
- Phone layout never scrolls. Fat targets.
- No placeholder art. The sparkle is PixelLab (approved, 11 Nov 2026).
- No function over 150 lines or a cognitive complexity of 15. Ceilings only come down.

## Review Focus

1. A rush running at the moment the phone locks: on return within the hour, it resumes with the same time left. After an hour, it is gone and a fresh quick vein comes.
2. The welcome-back card, a relic card, or the Buys drawer opens right as the quick vein would glint: the wait and the glint hold, and it glints once the board shows.
3. A rush runs while Overtime runs: the crew is ×20, the dial shows the Gold rush, and Running now lists both.
4. The mountain bottoms out mid-rush: the rush stops showing (`rushLeft` is 0 at `remaining === 0`), and accrue caps the stones as today.
5. A save from an older client, with no `rushUntil`, loads as no rush. A bad `rushUntil` is refused like a bad `axleUntil`.

### Task 1: The rush rules

**Files:**

- Create: `src/gold-rush.ts`, `src/gold-rush.test.ts`
- Modify: `src/mountain.ts` (the `Mountain` type gets `rushUntil?: number`; `accrueAway` calls `resumeRush`), `src/rates.ts` (`readRates` multiplies the timer), `src/rates.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**

- Produces:
  - `RUSH_MULTIPLIER = 10`, `RUSH_MS = 90_000`, `QUICK_VEIN_AWAY_MS = 3_600_000`.
  - `startRush(mountain: Mountain, now: number): Mountain` sets `rushUntil = now + RUSH_MS`.
  - `rushLeft(mountain: Mountain, now: number): number`: ms left, capped at `RUSH_MS`, and 0 when `remaining === 0`.
  - `rushMultiplier(mountain: Mountain, now: number | undefined): number`: `RUSH_MULTIPLIER` while `rushLeft > 0`, else 1. It is 1 when `now` is undefined.
  - `resumeRush(mountain: Mountain, from: number, to: number): Mountain`: if a rush was running at `from` and `to - from < QUICK_VEIN_AWAY_MS`, push `rushUntil` later by `to - from`. If the gap is an hour or more, drop the field. A rush already over drops the field.

- [ ] Write the tests in `gold-rush.test.ts`. A started rush has 90,000 ms left and multiplier 10. With no `now` the multiplier is 1. At `remaining: 0` `rushLeft` is 0. `resumeRush` over a 5-minute gap keeps the same time left. Over a 61-minute gap it removes `rushUntil`. An ended rush is removed. In `rates.test.ts`, a crew with Overtime and a rush runs at ×20 `crewPerSecond`; `tapYield` is unchanged by the rush; `effectiveRates(m)` with no clock ignores it.
- [ ] Run `npx vitest run src/gold-rush.test.ts src/rates.test.ts` and see them fail.
- [ ] Implement. In `readRates`, the timer becomes `liveCrewTimer(mountain, now) * rushMultiplier(mountain, now)`. In `accrueAway`, apply `resumeRush(mountain, lastSeen, now)` before paying, so the store and the lab both get it.
- [ ] Run the tests and `npm run gate -- fast`. Commit.
