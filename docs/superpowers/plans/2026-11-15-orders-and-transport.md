## Rulings (agent-run sitting)

1. **Rows, not ids.** A building is a row of `BUILDING_ROWS` and a vehicle a row of `VEHICLE_ROWS`. What is special to one (its steps, its power, its price, what it carries, how many are given) is a field on its row, as #561 asks. Part 1 has one of each: the Light Tower (steps of 150K, 1.5M and 6M stone; its power is a night shift output of 45%) and the horse wagon (500 stone, ×1.219, carries 25 goods a second, the first one given). The Warehouse, the Foundry and trucks need brick and cut stone, which come with parts 2 and 3, so their rows come with those parts.
2. **The project is a union:** `unordered`, `delivering` or `onHold`. The last two carry the site: the building, its step (0 foundation, 1 structure, 2 roof) and what has arrived of each good. The next building is the first row that is not operational. With none left there is nothing to order.
3. **One order for the whole building.** When a step's goods have all arrived, the next step starts in the same second, with nothing taken back. When the roof's goods are in, the building is operational and the project is `unordered` again. The next building waits for his order.
4. **Delivery** happens in `accrue`, after the crew has dug. Transport carries at most its rate times the share times the whole seconds. It carries only what the pocket holds and only what the step still requires, and it steps on through the building with what is left of its capacity. A step that requires two goods splits the capacity evenly, and what one good cannot use goes to the other. Live, the share is 1. Unattended, it is the night shift output (rule 7.11).
5. **A fresh mine's night.** The mine digs for the first eight hours of the night at the night shift output (the issue: "the clock stops the mine at eight hours"; today's six full hours and then a slope stay in today's game). Transport delivers from the pocket for the whole night at the night shift output. When a roof goes on during the night, the night is split at that second, and the rest of it runs at the new output. No expedition, relic or Gold rush is in a fresh mine, so its night needs none of their splits.
6. **Night shift output** is 30% (`AWAY_PAY_RATE`), or the highest power of an operational building: 45% with the Light Tower. Part 1's Light Tower opens nothing else (spec, Until part 2 is live).
7. **The limit** of a delivering order is `transport` or a good, with the goods a second and the seconds the step has left at this rate. The good's income is the crew's speed. On hold, the limit is `onHold`. A good limits when the pocket runs dry before the step is done and the crew digs less than transport carries.
8. **The moves are actions:** `placeOrder`, `holdOrder`, `resumeOrder` and `buyVehicle`, listed only in a fresh mine. Hold and resume are free and instant. `valueOf` gives them no income gain (payback Infinity): teaching the lab's dads to order is #655. They are logged as `order.place`, `order.hold`, `order.resume` and `vehicle.buy`.
9. **The save gains facts and no version.** `fleet` (counts by vehicle id), `project` (only while ordered) and the mark `operational` are written only when they differ from today's empty values, so a save from today's game is unchanged. The reader refuses an impossible project as `bad`: a building that is operational or is not the next one, a step past the roof, a good the step does not require, or goods that have all arrived for a step (a complete step has already given way to the next). The ledger gains the new lines. Part 1's switch-on still resets the save (#664).
10. **The fleet starts with one horse wagon** in a fresh mine (rule 7.6). Today's game owns none.
