**Architecture:**

- **Pure rules with unit tests.** A new module, `src/ledger-rows.ts`, turns the field log ring, the mountain and the auth state into `RowView`s (`src/row-view.ts`) and plain line views, as `buys-rows.ts`, `upgrade-rows.ts` and `workshop-rows.ts` do. It imports no art and no DOM. It replaces `src/screen-ledger.ts`, which goes.
- **Shared drawer pieces, extended** (each named in Task 1):
  - `board-drawer.ts` gains `BoardSheet.showPage(page)`: one panel page in the list's place, under the head and over Back, with the settle started again. It is `showHow`'s mechanism for a panel's own screen.
  - `board-row.ts` gains an `iconPx` option (32, 48 or 64), and takes the progress bar from `workshop-parts.ts` as `rowBar()` and `paintRowBar()`, so the Workshop bench and the Ledger's relic row share one bar.

  - `board-how-art.ts` learns the Time away and Relics cards.

- **The panel.** A new `src/ledger-panel.ts` is a thin builder on `createBoardSheet` and `rowsOnBoard`, with every function under 150 lines and complexity 15. `src/menu-panel.ts` and its test are deleted. Its presses go through the model (`pressOnBoard`, `rowsOnBoard.press`), so the settle, the hold and the blink are B5's.
- **Wiring.**
  - The rail key opens the drawer itself (`screen-keys.ts`). `wireKeys` closes every other drawer from one list, instead of naming each pair.
  - The drawer joins `drawers` in `mountain-screen.ts`, so the sheet clip, the scrim (`screen-layout.ts`) and `overlays.drawers` take it.
  - The menu, its confirm and the field log sheet leave `cardsFrom` (`screen-panels.ts`).
  - `legacy-panel.ts` loses `menuPass` and gains `offered()`. `saved-copies-panel.ts` loses `entry` and gains `open()`. The Ledger's plates call them.
  - `workshop-panel.ts` loses `menuEntry`, `createMenuEntry` and `onMenuOpen`.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vite, Vitest (happy-dom per file), puppeteer-core scripts (`scripts/visual-check.mjs` and its `scripts/visual/*.mjs`, `scripts/play.mjs`, `scripts/ui-audit.mjs`).

## Review Focus

1. **A sleepy double tap.**
   - Key, then the row or plate under the finger: nothing signs out, nothing copies, no page opens.
   - Start over, then the plate under the finger on its page: nothing is thrown away. `Yes, start over` acts only past the page's settle.
   - A copied field log holds its plate (`Copied`) for 1.9s, and a second press in that time does nothing.
2. **Start over still resets everything, and only from its page.** The run, the vein wait (`onStartOver`) and the drawer close. Focus goes to the Ledger key.
3. **A save that loads again under the open drawer.** The stones and relics rows go dark, and the field log and Start over plates disable. Account stays live, as today. If a page is up, it stays; `Yes, start over` is disabled.
4. **The doors.**
   - The Ledger key is the one door. Back, the scrim and Escape close it, on the list or on a page.
   - Opening any other drawer closes the Ledger, and the Ledger closes them.
   - Saved copies and Pass the mine close the Ledger, then open today's cards.
   - The menu's Workshop row is gone. The Workshop key still opens the Workshop, and its Back still falls back to the Ledger key when the Workshop key is disabled.
5. **Tonight never claims what did not happen.** A line is shown only for an event in the ring after the run started, and an event it cannot word is skipped, never guessed.

Added by this plan:

- **Tonight is this run's field log, newest three lines.** The ring (`progress.fieldLog().events`) is per open and is never a save field, which is what "tonight" means. A Start over or a pass starts a new Tonight. Each event is worded by one table (Task 2), or skipped. Save, log and housekeeping codes are never shown: the save line speaks for the save. When nothing is worded, Tonight's legend and box hide and the spare plate fills, so no empty-state copy is needed.
- **Three lines, not the mockup's four.** It is what fits (budget below). Each line is one line with an ellipsis, never two.
- **The mine is two rows.**

  - **Relics:** the next relic's dark icon at 48 (the mockup's), `2 of 6 relics found`, today's goal words, the next-layer hint as the third line, and today's guide bar at the row's foot, on the shared row bar. Once every relic is found, the lit Mantle and today's done sentence.

The label goes on the info line, which ellipsizes, so a long email never pushes the name. A tap while signing in does nothing and does not blink. ## Risks

- **The 538px budget is tight** (18px spare). The drawer's own fit check proves it on every save at every size. If the doors grid overflows on a PC size, the plates drop to 48px (still a target), named in the PR. No line wraps.
- **Tonight's copy** waits on Flemming's answer. Options 2 and 3 only remove rows of the table or the box, so the build does not change shape.
- **The materials line at 13px** with four materials (`Clay 1.2K · Iron 840 · Crystal 35 · Heartstone 2`) may not fit 250px. It ellipsizes; the `heart` flow and play run show where. The full text is on the row's `aria-label`.
- **Clipboard in headless Chrome** may refuse or allow. The states flow stubs a refusal to see the page; the play run takes whichever Chrome gives.
- **Test churn** in `mountain-screen.test.ts` (42 references) and four screen suites is the largest cost of Task 4. The harness helpers keep each change to a selector or one call.
- **Size.** About 6–7 agent hours: Task 4 is about 3, and the others are about 1 each. If Task 4 runs long, the ui-audit retarget (not in the gate) can move to its own small follow-up, named in the PR. No other part may.
