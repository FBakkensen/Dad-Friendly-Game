## The control board (#370)

The board's art is PixelLab art approved with the control-board mockup in B1. It is copied, not moved, from `mockups/control-board/assets/art/ind-board/` (and its `anim/` strips) into `src/assets/board/`, so the mockup still opens. The gauge row (#371) adds eight PixelLab files approved with B3 (marked "Approved B3" below; they are not in the mockup) and copies the rest of the window's and the gauge's art from the mockup, `mine-view.png` as `mine-view-granite.png`. The bays and the Ready row (#372) copy their art from the mockup with the names kept, and add one PixelLab file approved with B4, `mach-machine.png` (marked "Approved B4" below). One copy is not the mockup's: `plate.png` has its dark flecks cleared under the text window, so the Ready now lines read at 4.5:1. Flemming approved that edit in B4. Do not re-copy `plate.png` from the mockup, or the flecks come back. The key rail and the Buys and Upgrades drawers (#373) copy their icons, the worker and the drill, the maker's plate and strip and the stone icon from the mockup with the names kept, and add six PixelLab files approved with B5 (marked "Approved B5" below): `res-pick.png`, `res-mountain.png`, `mach-kid.png`, `mach-kid-dim.png`, `mach-machine-dim.png` and `key-dot.png`. The Workshop drawer (#374) copies `res-clay.png` and `res-iron.png` from the mockup and adds two PixelLab files, `res-crystal.png` and `res-heart.png`, for its material plates. `mach-kid-dim.png` is `mach-kid.png` recoloured into the dim key icons' greys, since the board allows no CSS filters. The board shows it at exactly 2× (one art pixel is two CSS pixels, `--art`), `image-rendering: pixelated`, with no CSS filters. Sizes are in art pixels.
