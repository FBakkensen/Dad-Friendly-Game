## The control board (#370)

The board's art is PixelLab art approved with the control-board mockup in B1. It is copied, not moved, from `mockups/control-board/assets/art/ind-board/` (and its `anim/` strips) into `src/assets/board/`, so the mockup still opens. The gauge row (#371) adds eight PixelLab files approved with B3 (marked "Approved B3" below; they are not in the mockup) and copies the rest of the window's and the gauge's art from the mockup, `mine-view.png` as `mine-view-granite.png`. The bays and the Ready row (#372) copy their art from the mockup with the names kept, and add one PixelLab file approved with B4, `mach-machine.png` (marked "Approved B4" below). One copy is not the mockup's: `plate.png` has its dark flecks cleared under the text window, so the Ready now lines read at 4.5:1. Flemming approved that edit in B4. Do not re-copy `plate.png` from the mockup, or the flecks come back. The key rail and the Buys and Upgrades drawers (#373) copy their icons, the worker and the drill, the maker's plate and strip and the stone icon from the mockup with the names kept, and add six PixelLab files approved with B5 (marked "Approved B5" below): `res-pick.png`, `res-mountain.png`, `mach-kid.png`, `mach-kid-dim.png`, `mach-machine-dim.png` and `key-dot.png`. The Workshop drawer (#374) copies `res-clay.png` and `res-iron.png` from the mockup and adds two PixelLab files, `res-crystal.png` and `res-heart.png`, for its material plates. The Engineers drawer (#455) adds three PixelLab files, `res-map.png`, `res-boot.png` and `res-nugget.png`, for the steps of its Engineers and Expeditions How it works cards. The relics drawer (#465) adds five PixelLab keepsake icons, `keepsake-<keepsake>.png`, in the material icons' style, in place of the old gold nugget. `mach-kid-dim.png` is `mach-kid.png` recoloured into the dim key icons' greys, since the board allows no CSS filters. The board shows it at exactly 2× (one art pixel is two CSS pixels, `--art`), `image-rendering: pixelated`, with no CSS filters. Sizes are in art pixels.

The six board cards (#464) add no file. They reuse the board's art, all at exactly 2×: `frame.png` for each card's face; `inset.png` and `lamp.png` (off, or green on the side picked) for the save conflict's two sides; the new band's `mine-view-*.png` on the layer card, at 184×152; on craft done, the fitted thing's board icon (`mach-cart.png` for iron rails, `mach-drill.png` for diamond bits, `mach-machine.png` for the heartstone core and ember, `mach-worker.png` for the engineer's kit, at 64; `relic-lantern.png` for the crystal lamp and flare, at 48; `res-stone.png` for sturdy props, the blasting charge, reinforce and axle grease, at 32); and on the welcome card's material line, the first material's `res-<material>.png` at 32 (`workshop-parts.ts#MATERIAL_ART`). `key.png` skins their plates. Today's card art (`mountain.png` on the layer card, `ore-lump.png` and the old tier sprites) left the cards; the files stay for the mockups.

The board's type is bundled, not loaded from Google: Barlow Condensed 500, 600 and 700 for words and Share Tech Mono 400 for the instruments' numbers (the odometer, flaps, gauge, dial and crew count), latin woff2 only, copied from the `@fontsource/barlow-condensed` and `@fontsource/share-tech-mono` packages (5.x) into `src/assets/fonts/`. Both are under the SIL Open Font License; the notices are `src/assets/fonts/OFL-barlow-condensed.txt` and `src/assets/fonts/OFL-share-tech-mono.txt`. `src/board.css` declares them with `font-display: block`. A figure on a plate (a price) is Barlow Condensed 700 at 17px with tabular figures and a drawer row's gain is the same face at 16px, and a rate or effect line is 15px semi-bold in the bright ink (#505): the thin mono only reads at 20px and up.
